# Research Summary

## Assignment
Identify and structure a shortlist of potential business-service providers from publicly available information.

## Process
1. Define the fields required before researching.
2. Capture company, industry, location and website information consistently.
3. Verify each source before recording the result.
4. Separate verified facts from interpretation.
5. Present the results in a concise format that a manager can review quickly.

## Quality control
Every row includes a source-check field so the research process is auditable.
