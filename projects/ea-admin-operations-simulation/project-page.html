Executive Assistant & Administrative Operations Simulation

A realistic administrative workflow showing how I would triage an executive inbox, protect priorities, coordinate a calendar, write professional responses, track commitments and turn meeting information into actionable follow-up.

What I demonstrated

Inbox Management

18 messages classified by urgency, importance and required action using Delegate, Alert, Respond, Schedule or Archive decisions.

Calendar Coordination

Meeting priorities, preparation requirements, schedule changes and potential conflicts were identified and documented.

Follow-through

Important work was converted into owners, deadlines, dependencies, status and follow-up dates.

Workflow

Inbox → Prioritise → Decide → Respond / Delegate → Track → Follow up → Brief

The goal is to make administrative information actionable. Each important commitment should have a clear next step, responsible owner and follow-up point.
