# Executive Assistant & Administrative Operations Simulation

## Portfolio Demonstration

A practical simulation demonstrating inbox management, prioritisation, calendar coordination, formal business communication, action tracking and executive reporting.

### What this project demonstrates

- Inbox triage across 18 realistic business messages
- Delegate / Alert / Respond / Schedule / Archive decisions
- Urgency versus importance assessment
- Executive escalation and decision-point identification
- Calendar coordination and conflict awareness
- Action-item ownership, deadlines and follow-up
- Formal business email writing
- Meeting notes converted into actionable tasks
- Concise executive briefing
- Structured administrative documentation

### Project workflow

**Inbox → Prioritise → Decide → Respond / Delegate → Track → Follow up → Brief**

The objective is to ensure that important commitments have a clear owner, next step, deadline and follow-up point.

> I built this simulation to demonstrate how I would support an executive in a real administrative environment. I separated urgent matters from routine communication, assigned clear actions, protected the executive's time, tracked deadlines and converted scattered information into a concise daily brief.
