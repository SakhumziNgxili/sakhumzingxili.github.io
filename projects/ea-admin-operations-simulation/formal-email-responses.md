# Formal Email Response Examples

These examples demonstrate professional written communication for common Executive Assistant and Administrative Support situations.

## 1. Marketing Agency — Webinar Graphics

**Subject:** Re: Draft Webinar Graphics for Review

Dear Marketing Team,

Thank you for sending through the draft webinar graphics.

I confirm receipt and will coordinate the review. I will provide consolidated feedback by Friday and will let you know if any additional information is required before then.

Kind regards,
Sakhumzi Ngxili
Administrative Support

## 2. Executive Coach — Coaching Call Agenda

**Subject:** Re: Tomorrow's Coaching Call Agenda

Dear [Coach Name],

Thank you for sending the agenda for tomorrow's coaching call.

I confirm receipt and will ensure the agenda is available ahead of the session. I will also prepare the relevant notes template so that key discussion points, decisions and follow-up actions can be recorded during the call.

Kind regards,
Sakhumzi Ngxili
Executive Assistant / Administrative Support

## 3. Potential Partner — Introductory Meeting

**Subject:** Re: Request for an Introductory Meeting

Dear [Name],

Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in arranging an introductory meeting.

I would be pleased to coordinate a suitable time. The following options are currently available:

- Wednesday at 15:00
- Thursday at 14:00

Please let me know which option is most convenient for you, and I will arrange the calendar invitation accordingly.

Kind regards,
Sakhumzi Ngxili
Administrative Support

## 4. Sales Lead — Pipeline Review Rescheduled

**Subject:** Re: Sales Pipeline Review — Updated Schedule

Dear [Name],

Thank you for the update.

I confirm that the Sales Pipeline Review has been moved to Thursday at 11:00. I will update the calendar invitation and ensure that the latest pipeline information is available ahead of the meeting.

Please let me know if there are any additional agenda items that should be included.

Kind regards,
Sakhumzi Ngxili
Administrative Support

## 5. Team Member — Annual Leave Request

**Subject:** Re: Annual Leave Request

Dear [Name],

Thank you for submitting your annual leave request.

I will review the relevant dates against the team calendar and confirm whether adequate coverage is available. I will provide an update once the calendar and operational requirements have been checked.

Kind regards,
Sakhumzi Ngxili
Administrative Support

## 6. Travel Coordinator — Conference Flight Options

**Subject:** Re: Conference Flight Options

Dear [Travel Coordinator Name],

Thank you for providing the available conference flight options.

I confirm receipt and will place the preferred option on hold where possible while executive confirmation is obtained. I will provide an update once a final decision has been received.

Kind regards,
Sakhumzi Ngxili
Administrative Support
