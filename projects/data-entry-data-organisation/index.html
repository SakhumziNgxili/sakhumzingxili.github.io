Data Entry & Data Organisation

A self-created demonstration of accurate data capture, spreadsheet organisation, data-quality checking and basic business reporting.

View Sample DatasetProject Summary

Project Objective

Organise a realistic fictional customer/order dataset so that information can be reviewed, searched, filtered and used for routine administrative work.

What I Demonstrated

  • Accurate data capture and structured record keeping
  • Consistent headings, dates and currency formatting
  • Controlled status values for easier filtering
  • Reviewing records for missing or inconsistent information
  • Sorting, filtering and preparing data for basic summaries

Business Scenario

A fictional small business needs customer and order records maintained in a clear, consistent format. The dataset contains 20 fictional records and includes customer, order, product, payment and status information.

Important Note

This is a portfolio demonstration. All names, contact details and business records are fictional and were created for skills demonstration purposes. It is not represented as paid client work.
